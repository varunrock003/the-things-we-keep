Detailed Description

Steam verb for the kettle. Listens to the PossessableObject on the same GameObject: possess the kettle with E, press Space, and it steams.

The look of the steam itself lives on the SteamFX particle system. This script controls when it emits, tapers the emission off at the end, and adds a lid rattle and warm tint on the kettle so the verb reads from the fixed camera. Pressing Space while steaming restarts the timer.
